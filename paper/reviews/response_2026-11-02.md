# Response to the internal referee report of 2026-11-02

Point by point. "Done" means the text was changed in `paper/exact_graphstate_sampling.md` (and, where the same
claim appears there, in `graphsamp/README.md`, `FINDINGS.md`, `outreach/`, `submission/`, `results/nq80/README.md`
and the figure labels) in the revision of 2 November 2026.

## Major issues

**M1 (80-qubit samples called exact).** Accepted in full. The 80-qubit run used the hybrid sampler (60 bits proposed
uniformly and accepted with probability z/1.03, 20 bits drawn exactly), so its output is the Born law conditioned on
z ≤ 1.03. We did not compute the second moment of the prefix marginal over all 2^{60} prefixes, so the excluded mass
has no rigorous bound; the Gaussian/Porter–Thomas estimate (cap at 30 standard deviations) is now labelled as a model
estimate. Done: abstract item 2, the results table, §2 (the sentence contrasting with #260 now says where the chain
rule is used and where the hybrid sampler replaces it), §3.4 (rewritten; states K = 60, why the chain rule was not
used with a sliced table, the conditioning, the absence of a rigorous bound, and that the re-scoring used the same
engine), §7 (the sampler paragraph; the difference from #260 is now "one of degree"), §8, the README table and run
bullet, FINDINGS, the one-pager, the submission draft, `results/nq80/README.md`, and the Figure 1 label ("sampled
(hybrid, capped)"). The rigorous route the referee suggests (exact Var(z) by enumerating the stabilizers inside the
prefix, then Chebyshev) is recorded as the next step if the 80-qubit result is to be called exact; note that the
cross terms between distinct stabilizers of equal support also enter Var(z) for a product basis with all three Bloch
components nonzero, so the enumeration is over pairs, not elements.

**M2 (contradiction on the doped instance).** Accepted. The two stale sentences (§5 caveats, §8 "does not show")
are replaced; §8 "shows" now lists the doped amplitudes; the abstract has a fourth item on the doped instance and
the results table a row for it.

**M3 (numbers with no committed artifact).** Accepted. All `test_*.py` scripts were run on 2 November 2026 and their
printed output committed under `results/tests/`; §3.4, §4 item 1 and §7 now point there. The "1.5× per sliced
vertex" sentence is removed (no same-machine measurement exists) and the caveat says so. The header sentence now
includes the test outputs in its promise. A Porter–Thomas test of the 1,012 samples was added as a script and an
artifact (`porter_thomas_test.py`, `results/porter_thomas_nq70.txt`, and `results/nq80/porter_thomas_nq80.txt`).

**M4 (low-order null results over-interpreted).** Accepted. The abstract and §6 now state the detection power (a
marginal departing from ½ by about 4 percentage points at 3σ with N = 1,726), say that a 1% per-qubit error rate is
below it, and say "consistent with global depolarisation but does not establish it". §8 says "no low-order bias
above the 4-point level that 1,726 shots can detect". The Figure 4 title is unchanged (it states the test, not the
interpretation).

**M5 (frontier gap and the n = 80 row).** Accepted. §5 now says the gap is n/2 − 4 to n/2 − 3 at the frontier
budget and n/2 − 5 on the three instances searched longer (widths 30, 33, 35), that the gap at the budgets used is
3–5 and the true linear rank-width is unknown, and that the tightness of the ceiling is open. The n = 80 row now
gives 2^{40.3} (from `fi80_s5.log`, log₂ Σ 2^{r_k} = 38.29) and the Figure 1 caption gives the same. The abstract,
§5, §8, FINDINGS and the one-pager say 3–5 and "a factor of about 30", and "the only lever" became "the main lever".

**M6 (node size unstated).** Accepted. The abstract and §5 now give the ceiling's memory at 76, 80, 82, 90 and 100
qubits and state the thresholds against explicit sizes: a 2 TB node to n = 75, a 24–32 TB node (the largest sold
today) to n = 83, 281 TB to n = 90; the construction's fitted width fits a 32 TB node to about 90 qubits. The
Figure 1 band label is unchanged ("one cloud node (up to 512 GB)") and the caption now names that size.

**M7 (fidelity model dependence).** Accepted. §3.1 reports the Porter–Thomas test of the 1,012 samples (KS 0.016
to z·e^{−z}, 5% level 0.043; mean z² 6.3 ± 0.3; mean log₂ Pr −69.398 ± 0.038 against −69.390), §3.2 states that
the mixture MLE assumes Exp(1) over strings and points to the synthetic F = 1 case for the residual bias, and the
"unlike the Clifford instance" clause in §7 is corrected. The abstract already carried the caveat that the
quantities differ ("Two fidelity estimators ... above IBM's direct-fidelity estimate") in §3.2's words; the abstract
sentence itself was left as a statement of the numbers, with the interpretation confined to §3.2.
